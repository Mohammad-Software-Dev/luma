# Amber crystal artwork

Created 7 October 2026 with the built-in image generation tool, using the imagegen skill. No commercial game sprites were extracted.

Final runtime asset: `public/art/environment-crystals.webp` (1254 × 1254, alpha preserved, WebP quality 88). The four source rectangles in `app/environment.ts` select the narrow seal, arena cluster, shattered stump, and regrowing sprouts. All states share a ground anchor; the compressed atlas is loaded once and drawn without per-frame filters.

## Full generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent sprite atlas for Luma, a richly painted realistic fantasy side-view platform game. Create a 2 by 2 atlas of four isolated natural amber crystal formations. Top left: tall narrow intact seal, rough irregular honey-colored quartz growing out of mossy dark basalt roots. Top right: wider intact arena formation, several uneven interlocking translucent amber crystals. Bottom left: shattered low stump with scattered amber shards, same basalt footing. Bottom right: small regrowing crystal sprouts from the same footing. Each object centered in its own equal square cell with generous transparent margins, all footings near bottom of their cell, no overlap. Detailed hand-painted realism, organic weathered surfaces, internal amber glow and reflected golden light, deep brown shadows, tiny moss details. Side elevation, game prop, readable irregular silhouette. Actual transparent background, no environment, no labels, no text, no watermark, no flat vector shapes or cartoon outlines.
