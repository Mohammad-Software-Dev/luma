# Boss action art — 6 October 2026

Generated in built-in image-generation mode, using Luma's existing `public/art/bosses.webp` as the identity reference. No commercial game character assets were used.

Two transparent 1536×1024 source sheets were isolated by alpha and packed into two 2048×1200 WebP atlases: `public/art/boss-actions-a.webp` (Briarhorn, Tidewing, Amberback) and `public/art/boss-actions-b.webp` (Gale Sovereign, Moonbloom, Solwarden). `app/boss-action-atlas.ts` records source rectangles and anatomical pivots. Four poses per guardian cover movement, anticipation, release, and recovery. The renderer blends transitions and adds recoil, compression, and wing motion.

## Sheet A prompt

Use case: identity-preserve. Create a transparent combat animation atlas for the FIRST THREE bosses in the reference image only, keeping their exact recognizable materials, anatomy, colors and painterly realistic rendering. Reference is character design reference, not a sheet layout to preserve. Output 1536x1024, precisely 4 columns by 3 rows, each cell 384x341, each entire creature inside its own cell with at least 20px transparent padding and no overlap. Consistent creature size and feet baseline within each row, facing RIGHT in side/three-quarter view. Row 1 is Briarhorn, the mossy bronze rhinoceros beetle with large wooden antlers, flowers and cream horn. Four columns: (1) alert striding with front leg raised, (2) deep crouch braced with lowered forward horn before charge, (3) forceful airborne forward charge with legs tucked and antlers trailing, (4) exhausted open-armoured recovery stance head low front legs spread and amber chest visible. Row 2 is Tidewing, the blue aquatic owl with white face and curling water feathers. Columns: (1) rising flight with wings raised high, (2) wings tightly drawn back building a spell, (3) powerful full downward wing stroke casting forward, (4) landing recovery wings drooping and feet lowered. Row 3 is Amberback the dark basalt beetle studded with amber crystals and angular golden horn. Columns: (1) deliberate heavy forward step, (2) braced crouch crushing front legs into earth, (3) rearing violent upward crystal eruption pose with forelegs raised, (4) low resting posture with armour plates parted and glowing core visible. Make these significantly distinct action poses, not recolors or tiny rotations of idle. Character artwork only: no effects obscuring bodies, no ground, no text, no grids, no background, true transparent alpha.
