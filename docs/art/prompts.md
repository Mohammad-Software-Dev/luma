# Combat art direction and generation prompts

Created 6 October 2026 with the **built-in image generation tool**, with transparent-background output enabled. Hero generations used `public/guardian.png` as the character reference. The generated assets are shipped as WebP in `public/art/`; none depend on a path outside the repository.

Visual direction: detailed, painterly fantasy materials that fit the existing cinematic forest backgrounds. Preserve Luma's cream fur, oversized ears, amber eyes, orange scarf, and joyful personality. Make the character's weight transfer, wind-up, release, and recovery readable. Keep effects separate from sprites so hit timing, direction, accessibility, and brightness remain under engine control.

## Hero combat atlas

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: production transparent combat sprite sheet for the side-scrolling game Luma. Reference image is the existing Luma character: preserve its cream fur, huge swept ears, amber eyes, orange scarf, proportions, detailed painterly 3D lighting. Create ONE transparent PNG sprite atlas, landscape 1536x1024, exactly FOUR equal columns and THREE equal rows, twelve frames. Each cell 384x341 approximately, absolutely no grid lines, no text, no scenery or shadows on a ground plane. One full-body Luma per cell, all facing RIGHT, feet at consistent baseline at 88% of each cell, body center near horizontal 42%, consistent head/body size across every frame; leave margin for sword and ears, do not overlap cells. Luma wields a slender curved golden SUNLIGHT BLADE conjured from its right paw, organic luminous leaf-shaped energy saber (not a metal sword). Row1 left-to-right four distinct anatomically credible poses for forward horizontal slash: 1 planted crouch with arm and blade drawn behind left shoulder, 2 arm accelerating blade in front at chest height weight stepping into right foot, 3 full forward extension with torso rotated and scarf trailing, 4 relaxed balanced return with blade lowered. Row2 four poses for rising backhand slash: low coil with bent knees; sweeping arm diagonally up; high follow-through with blade overhead; balanced recovery. Row3 four poses for powerful overhead finisher: both paws lift blade overhead and weight back; downward committed swing; low forward lunge with blade diagonal toward ground at right; recover standing. Show the hands actually gripping/conjuring the blade and feet bracing the movement, expressive focused determined face, real body weight transfer. Keep blade thin and clean, NO large baked-in slash arcs, no particles, no motion blur (effects are added in engine). Polished high-detail hand-painted fantasy character, warm rim lighting, fine fur and cloth, designed to read at 100px tall. Transparent background and clean alpha, no checkerboard.

## Integration notes

Generated layouts were measured rather than assumed to match the requested grid. Creature and boss silhouettes were packed into separate atlas cells; pivots keep bodies registered across poses. Hero combat frames use measured row boundaries and planted-foot pivots. The eight-frame run sequence remains the existing artwork. Kenney's CC0 particle textures provide the reusable effects; see `public/art/CREDITS.md` and its original license file.

Combat timing: 45ms anticipation, an active swing ending at 205ms, and recovery through 290ms. A three-hit combination uses horizontal, rising, and downward arcs. Contact bursts occur on actual damage, guards, and parries. Reduced motion removes dash ghosts, extra spark travel, and wing deformation while preserving attack poses and warnings. Effects and afterimages have fixed caps and clear on travel, death, and new journeys.
